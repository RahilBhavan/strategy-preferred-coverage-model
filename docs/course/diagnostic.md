# Diagnostic: what do you already understand?

Closed book, 25–35 minutes. Calculator permitted; no answer key or assistant. All amounts are hypothetical dollars. Explain each answer, not only the number.

1. A company has assets 200, debt 80 and preferred stated claims 40. What is the simplified common residual? Is it necessarily the common stock's market value?
2. It issues 30 of common stock and retains the cash. What changes in assets, cash and operating revenue?
3. Its BTC rises in value by 25 without any sale. Which item changes: asset value, cash, or both?
4. What is the present value of 121 received in two years at a 10% annual discount rate?
5. A perpetual pays 8 each year, first payment in one year. At a constant 10% required return, what is its model price? Name one simplifying assumption.
6. Net assets are 120 and there are 60 common shares. Issue 10 shares at 3 and retain the proceeds. Calculate the new net assets per share.
7. A preferred has a 100 stated claim, is issued for 75, and pays 9 annually. Calculate annual cash payout divided by gross proceeds. Is that the full economic cost?
8. A convertible pays no coupon and is issued at par. Give two economic costs or risks that still exist.
9. Available cash is 40; annual distributions are 10; a 35 note put falls due in six months. Does “four years of distribution coverage” prove the company can meet all obligations?
10. An issuer says a transaction increases net BTC per diluted share. Name two additional questions before concluding it creates economic value.

Score with the separate key: two points each. One point for the correct numerical/factual result and one for the explanation or limit. For Q8 give one point for each valid distinct cost/risk; for Q10 give one for each distinct additional question.

16–20: attempt the foundation gates; skip only readings you can demonstrate you do not need. 10–15: complete Units 1–4 in order. 0–9: begin Lesson 1 slowly and explain every transaction aloud. Any critical misconception in Q2, Q3 or Q9 needs correction regardless of total score.

These thresholds are course design choices, not validated measures of professional competence. Record the first result without replacing it with a corrected score.
