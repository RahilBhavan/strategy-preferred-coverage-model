# Economic plan: bounded primary-source check

Access date: **2026-10-23**. Reviewed `textbook-source/ch/e1.html`, `n1.html`, and `v1.html`. No quotes, balances, or market prices were refreshed. The following eight caveats should constrain the learning plan; they are not a replacement valuation.

1. **Financing is not earnings or cash generation.** `e1` says Strategy “makes money” by selling slices above bitcoin cost. Securities issuance raises financing and creates claims; it is not operating revenue. Separate software operating cash, financing receipts, BTC purchases/sales, unrealized fair-value earnings, and distributions. The 2025 10-K says unrealized BTC gains generate no cash and the software business did not generate positive operating cash in 2025. Those are historical disclosures, not an October 2026 cash forecast. [K: Item 1A, liquidity risks; Item 7; cash-flow statement.]

2. **Common issuance accretion needs a named metric and assumptions.** `e1`/`v1` conflate growing gross BTC/share, net BTC/share, and shareholder value. Current Notes define mNAV using Net BPS, deducting specified senior notionals net of USD Assets, and counting in-the-money conversions in fully diluted shares. Above-one issuance accretion assumes stable prices, BTC deployment, and hypothetical liquidation; fees, other deployment, omitted liabilities and subsequent financing change results. Below-one issuance can still occur: dilution is not inability to raise capital. Do not treat `n1`’s $0.16 per dollar as guaranteed shareholder profit. [N: “mNAV, Net BTC, Net BPS, and BPS ($)”.]

3. **Low convertible coupon is not low total economic cost.** `e1`’s “almost free” describes cash interest only. The June 2026 10-Q separately reports coupons, issuance costs/effective interest, conversion terms, puts and maturities. Teach the option granted to investors, contingent dilution, cash settlement and refinancing exposure. A stock-price endpoint does not alone determine contractual exercise. Buyer hedging and volatility profits are analytical possibilities, not established motives of every holder. Also, `n1`’s STRK calculation confuses conversion value with option value: 0.1 × stock price is immediate conversion value; the embedded option requires valuation. [Q: Note 4; S: §10.]

4. **Perpetual means no scheduled maturity, not “no repayment, ever.”** `n1`’s STRF description is too absolute. The 10-Q identifies issuer redemption and holder fundamental-change repurchase provisions. Model series-specific dividends, cumulative versus non-cumulative treatment, and consequences of nonpayment. STRK’s certificate includes compounding of arrears and conditional declaration requirements following common offerings; “board discretion” needs those qualifications. STRC’s rate is discretionary within its terms, not an automatic stress escalator or guaranteed price peg. [Q: Note 10; S: §5; C: §§5, 7–8; F: STRC policy.]

5. **Preferred stock is not secured BTC ownership.** `e1`’s “slices” and “borrows against” language obscures legal structure. Distinguish preferred equity priority from debt and a collateral lien. Q reports all BTC unencumbered at June 30, 2026; N denies security holders BTC ownership/redemption rights. A coverage ratio is neither earmarked collateral nor a guaranteed recovery floor. Custody, competing liabilities, realization costs and liquidation preferences matter. [Q: “Liquidity and Capital Resources”; N: KPI limitations.]

6. **An appreciation hurdle is a scenario, not cash coverage or investment break-even.** `e1`’s 2.2%/12% and `n1`’s dividend/net-proceeds ratios omit timing, terminal claims, changing rates, taxes, fees and discounting. A one-year mark-to-market surplus does not supply dividend cash. Keep cash carry, economic financing cost, issuer accretion and investor total return distinct. `v1`’s 1.27x sensitivity is local arithmetic under fixed assumptions, not a stock-return forecast. [Analytical conclusions; K: noncash-gain and liquidity disclosures.]

7. **Liquidity and refinancing are dynamic.** June 29’s framework permits BTC sales for reserve funding, distributions/interest and repurchases; it does not make sales a mandatory last resort. Its 12-month reserve minimum permits Board-authorized exceptions, and authorized monetization capacity is not cash already held. Coverage depends on rates, claims, prices, execution and financing access. Repurchase authorizations do not promise execution. [F: USD Reserve, monetization, repurchase sections.]

8. **Separate stated rationale, incentives and analyst inference; date every definition.** The framework states intended credit/liquidity and shareholder-value benefits; `e1`’s issuance matrix is analysis, not policy. Do not infer compensation motives without proxy evidence. N says mNAV changed July 23, 2026, and interim KPI methodology changed effective January 1 with later implementation/corrections. Label historical disclosures separately from current definitions. [F; N: methodology sections.]
