Appendix B

Glossary

Amplification
Strategy's word for leverage: using debt and preferreds so common holders get more than 1x bitcoin exposure.
ATM program
At-the-market offering: selling shares into the market gradually through brokers. Capacity is a ceiling, not guaranteed demand.
BTC cover
This book's term: bitcoin value ÷ (debt principal + preferred notional).
BTC Gain / BTC $ Gain
BTC Yield expressed in coins, or in dollars.
BTC Monetization Program
Board-approved rules for selling bitcoin: up to $1.25B into the Reserve, for dividends or interest when better than issuing stock, or to fund buybacks.
BTC Yield
% change in bitcoin per diluted share over a period. A company-defined measure, not GAAP.
CAMT
The US 15% corporate alternative minimum tax. Strategy said in Oct 2025 it no longer expects to owe it on unrealized bitcoin gains, after IRS interim guidance.
Capital stack
All claims on a company, ordered from first-paid (senior) to last-paid (common).
Carry
The yearly cost of the claims ahead of common, here about $1.56B, or 2.2% of the bitcoin's value.
Certificate of designation
The legal document that sets a preferred stock's terms: rate, ranking, what happens if dividends are skipped.
Convertible arbitrage
A hedge-fund strategy: buy a convertible, short the stock, profit from volatility. The main buyers of Strategy's converts.
Convertible note
Debt the holder can swap into shares at a set conversion price. Usually low coupon.
Cost of capital
What a company gives up to get money: interest, dividends, or a slice of future value for new shares.
Cumulative / non-cumulative
Cumulative: a skipped dividend is still owed (and may compound). Non-cumulative (STRD): it's simply gone.
Current yield
Annual dividend ÷ market price. Differs from the stated rate whenever the price isn't $100.
Digital credit
Strategy's name for its preferreds: fixed-income securities backed by its bitcoin.
Fair-value accounting
Marking bitcoin to market every quarter (ASU 2023-08, adopted 2025). Moves reported profit; produces no cash.
Fundamental-change put
A preferred holder's right to sell back at the stated amount after events such as a takeover.
Issuer call
Strategy's right to redeem a series: if under 25% remains, after a tax event, or for STRC at $101 any time.
Leverage
Using fixed claims to own more of an asset. Strategy's common owns about 1.27x bitcoin at net asset value.
Liquidation preference / stated amount / notional
The face value of a preferred, usually $100 a share (€100 for STRE). Dividends are a percentage of it.
LTV (loan-to-value)
Claims ÷ asset value. Used here for the bitcoin cushion beneath each preferred.
mNAV
Multiple of net asset value: how the market values the company relative to its bitcoin. Three versions: basic, enterprise value and net (Chapter 8).
NAV (net asset value)
Assets minus all claims ahead of common. For Strategy: bitcoin + cash − debt − preferred.
Net bitcoin per share
(Bitcoin + cash − debt − preferred, in bitcoin) ÷ fully diluted shares. The basis of net mNAV.
Pari passu
Ranking equally. None of Strategy's five preferreds are pari passu with each other.
Perpetual
Never matures. The issuer never has to repay principal but pays dividends indefinitely.
Put (holder put)
A convertible holder's right to demand repayment on a set date.
Reflexivity
When a price affects the thing it values. MSTR's premium enables the growth that justifies the premium.
Rotation
Selling one security to buy back another, e.g. selling MSTR to retire STRC.
Senior / junior
Who gets paid first. Debt is senior to all preferreds; STRF is senior to STRC, and so on down to STRD.
Shelf registration
An SEC filing that lets a company sell securities over time, e.g. through an ATM.
Stated amount
A preferred's face value ($100; €100 for STRE). Dividends are a percentage of it.
Step-up
STRF and STRE's penalty: if dividends go unpaid, the rate rises 1% per period up to 18%.
USD Reserve
Cash ring-fenced for dividends and interest. Policy: at least 12 months of charges.
