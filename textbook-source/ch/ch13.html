MARA credit case: the lender's view

Credit memo + decision engine + workbookmara.rahilbhavan.comHypothetical facility, public filings

The question. A bitcoin miner, MARA, asks for a $5.0M 12-month revolver secured by its bitcoin. How much should a lender approve, and on what conditions?

Strategy sells credit backed by bitcoin; this case is about buying it. It's the same skill from the other side of the table: how much is bitcoin worth as cover after a stress haircut, and what must be true before any money moves?

Facts from MARA's Jun 30, 2026 filing

  • 35,577 BTC, but 4,742 lent out and 4,528 pledged, leaving 26,307 free.
  • $421M of cash, about 30% held in a subsidiary for its own use.
  • About $2.4B of debt, including $291.6M that holders can put in June 2027. A static screen leaves −$194.8M if they do.

The link back to Strategy

Two lessons carry straight over. First, count only the coins and cash that are actually free: that's why the coverage model separates the ring-fenced Reserve from USD Cash. Second, watch put dates: MARA's June 2027 put is the same kind of risk as Strategy's $5.9B of puts in 2027–28.
