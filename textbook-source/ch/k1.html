Chapter 16

The credit view

A credit analyst asks two questions about any security that promises payments. Will the payments keep coming? And if they stop, how much of my money comes back?

The first is coverage: how long the cash lasts (Chapter 22 answers it in detail). The second is cushion: how much asset value sits below your claim. For Strategy's preferreds, the cushion is bitcoin.

How far bitcoin can fall before each layer is underwater

How that compares

A lender making a bitcoin-backed loan usually lends well under the coins' value and demands more collateral if the price falls. Strategy's most junior preferred sits at about 29% "LTV", which looks conservative by that standard. But the comparison flatters it in three ways:

  • No margin call means no early warning. A lender can sell collateral early. Preferred holders just watch.
  • One asset. The cushion is a single, volatile asset. Bitcoin has fallen more than 70% from its peak before.
  • Perpetual exposure. A one-year loan only has to survive one year. A perpetual preferred has to survive every year.

That's why S&P rated Strategy B- in October 2025, deep in high-yield territory. It cited bitcoin concentration, a narrow business, weak risk-adjusted capital and thin dollar liquidity. S&P rates the issuer. The preferreds, ranking below its debt, would be judged riskier still.

What the market says

The preferreds' trading prices are the market's running credit opinion. A preferred far below $100 is the market demanding a higher yield. STRC climbing back to about $99.65 by October 2026, from the high $80s in mid-2026, is the market saying the credit risk eased, helped by the 12% rate, buybacks and the larger Reserve (Chapter 11).

The credit analyst's summaryStrong cushion, strong near-term liquidity (37 months of Reserve), no maturities on the preferreds. Weaknesses: a single volatile asset, funding that depends on market confidence, and a junior position behind $6.8B of debt that holders can put in 2027–28. In short: well-cushioned, but with confidence-driven funding.

Check yourself

Why is STRD's cushion thinner than STRF's when both are backed by the same bitcoin?

STRD ranks last, so every claim above it (debt, STRF, STRC, STRE, STRK) is paid from the bitcoin first. Its claims-to-bitcoin ratio is 29% against STRF's 12%.
