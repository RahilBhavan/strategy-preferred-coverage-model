Chapter 14

Keeping score: Strategy's numbers

Strategy doesn't judge itself on earnings. It reports its own set of bitcoin measures, and it has changed some of them as the balance sheet changed. Know the definitions cold. The biggest trap is that "mNAV" has three different meanings.

Read with careDefinitions changed on July 23, 2026, and the BTC Yield/Gain methodology was updated in 2026. Compare only like definitions and dates. The timing does not establish management’s motive. See the issuer’s current definitions.

Check yourself

Can BTC Yield be positive while MSTR's price falls?

Yes. If Strategy sells stock at a premium, bitcoin per share rises even if bitcoin and the stock both fall. BTC Yield measures bitcoin per share, not value per share.

Why does net mNAV come out higher than basic mNAV?

The net version first subtracts debt and preferred from the bitcoin, which shrinks the denominator (net bitcoin per share). It also uses diluted shares and counts cash.
