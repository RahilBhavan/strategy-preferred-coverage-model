dat-accretion: one ruler for every capital action

Python + public pagev1 built; weekly refreshrahilbhavan.github.io/dat-accretion

The question. Strategy, BitMine and SharpLink each sell stock, buy it back and trade preferreds every week. Using Strategy's own net-coins-per-share definition for all three: what did each action do per dollar, and at what price does it change sign?

The key move is that Strategy's 2026 rotation (sell MSTR, retire STRC) and BitMine's opposite rotation (sell its BMNP preferred, buy back common) share one break-even line: m = q. Strategy's adds while m > q; BitMine's adds while m < q.

How it's built

Each weekly 8-K is parsed into actions (one row each, linked to its filing). Balances are rolled forward from 10-Q anchors, and each week's change in net coins per share is split into price, convert flips, each action, carry and a residual. A done check compares the rebuild against live sources before the page updates. There's also a 24-lesson course in the repo that teaches it from scratch.

What it taught me for this book

  • The rules table in Chapter 3.
  • Why net mNAV, not basic mNAV, is the number to watch.
  • That Strategy's 2026 buybacks were accretive on its own definition every week.

Core source: filing or issuer capital framework. Source dates and estimates are listed with the workbook inputs and in Appendix B.
