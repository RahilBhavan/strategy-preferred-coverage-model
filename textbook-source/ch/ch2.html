Chapter 4

How it got here

The story has three phases. From 2020 to 2024 Strategy bought bitcoin with common stock and cheap convertible debt. In 2025 it launched a preferred stock almost every few months. In 2026 the focus shifted to defending those preferreds: a cash reserve, a higher STRC rate, buybacks and new rules for when it will sell stock.

The patternEach new product answered a problem the last one created. Converts needed MSTR to keep rising; preferreds reached investors who wanted income; STRC's variable rate let Strategy adjust the price of money monthly; the Reserve answered "where do the dividends come from?"; buybacks and issuance discipline near 1x answered "what if the stock trades below its bitcoin?"
