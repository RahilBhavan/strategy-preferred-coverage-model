Chapter 9

What can break

Most risks start in the same place: the bitcoin price. A big fall hits Strategy through several channels at once, and they reinforce each other.

Outside pressures

Credit ratingS&P rated Strategy B- (Oct 2025), citing bitcoin concentration, a narrow business, weak risk-adjusted capital, thin dollar liquidity and the mismatch between bitcoin assets and dollar obligations.
Index treatmentIn Jan 2026 MSCI dropped a plan to exclude bitcoin-heavy companies but froze increases to their index share counts while it reviews "non-operating" companies. Forced selling estimates ran into the billions.
CurrencySTRE pays euros with no euro reserve. A 10% stronger euro adds about $9M a year. Small today, larger if STRE grows.
Control and key personSaylor's Class B shares carry about a third of the votes, enough to carry votes like the Oct 28 daily-dividend amendment with modest support from others.
The fair way to say itStrategy's design pushes risk toward common holders and away from the preferreds: years of cash in the Reserve, a long list of levers, and no principal due on the preferreds. The weak points are mNAV and STRC's price. Both depend on investor confidence, which can change faster than any cash buffer.
