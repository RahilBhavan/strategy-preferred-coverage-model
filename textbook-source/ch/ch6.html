STRC up close: a dividend that steers a price

STRC is the most important product to understand, for two reasons. It's the largest preferred, about two-thirds of Strategy's annual cash charges. Management can adjust its dividend rate monthly to encourage trading near $100. It remains perpetual preferred equity with market and payment risk. Rate changes require a board decision.

Three 2026 changes around STRC

Buybacks≈ 17m shares ($1.65B) retired from July to Oct 11, 2026, paid for by selling MSTR. Adds net bitcoin per share while STRC trades below $100 × net mNAV ($119 on Oct 11).
Twice-monthly, then dailyMoved from monthly to twice a month in June. If shareholders approve on Oct 28, STRC pays every calendar day from Nov 1 ($1.00 a share for November, $0.03–$0.04 a day).
Rate on hold at 12%Management says it won't recommend changes until STRC shows "sustained, healthy trading near $100."
The risk to understandA variable rate can increase funding costs during stress. The board retains discretion; the price bands do not mechanically reset the coupon. Bitcoin weakness, preferred prices and rate decisions can interact without moving in lockstep. My coverage model (Chapter 27) lets you push STRC to 15% and see what happens to the Reserve.

Check yourself

Why would daily dividends help STRC's price?

More frequent record and payment dates reduce the amount accruing between payments. Strategy argues that this may reduce price swings around dividend dates. It does not eliminate reasons to sell or guarantee price stability.

STRC is at $99 and MSTR's net mNAV is 1.2x. Does buying back STRC add bitcoin per share?

Yes. The rotation adds while m > q: 1.2 > 0.99. It would stop adding only if STRC traded above $120.
