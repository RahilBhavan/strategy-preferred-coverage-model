Chapter 9

Every product, one card each

Why sell five different preferreds instead of one? Because each one is built for a different buyer. Some want safety, some want the highest yield, some want a bond-like price that barely moves, some want euros, and some want a piece of the upside. Strategy's pitch is that it can sell "digital credit" to all of them, each on different terms, all backed by the same bitcoin.

Check yourself

Why does STRD pay more than STRK?

It's more junior and its dividends can be skipped forever. STRK is senior to it, its unpaid dividends are owed, and it converts into MSTR, which is worth something by itself.

Which preferred would Strategy most like to keep paying in a crisis, and why?

STRF (and STRE): skipping them triggers compounding at a rate that steps up to 18%. STRD is the one it could skip at no legal cost.
